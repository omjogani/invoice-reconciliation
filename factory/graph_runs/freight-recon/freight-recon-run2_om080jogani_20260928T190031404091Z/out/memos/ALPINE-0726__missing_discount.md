# ALPINE-0726 · Missing Volume Discount

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹1,007.46 volume discount owed
**What happened:** BlueFin tendered 40 consignments to Alpine Express in 2026-07, well above the 12-consignment threshold that triggers a 5% volume discount on that month's invoice. No discount was applied — the billed discount was ₹0.00.
**Contract basis:** §5 — "In any calendar month in which the Customer tenders more than 12 consignments, the Carrier will apply a 5% discount to the invoice total for that month's invoice."
**Recommended action:** Ask Alpine Express to apply the 5% volume discount and credit ₹1,007.46 against invoice ALPINE-0726. An additional small amount of discount depends on the separately escalated line ALPINE-0726#5 and should be settled once that item is resolved.
