# FALCON-2026-07A · FF-8011

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹1,200.00 detention charge at consignee, billed but not permitted under the contract
**What happened:** The invoice includes a ₹1,200.00 detention charge at consignee alongside freight and fuel surcharge that otherwise match the contract. The rate card does not list detention as a chargeable accessorial.

**Recommended action:** Ask Falcon to credit the ₹1,200.00 detention charge, or to produce a written rate confirmation authorizing it under §5.
**Deadline:** The §7 dispute window closes 2026-08-28 and is closed as of 2026-09-29. The invoice date is not printed; the deadline assumes the end of the billing period (2026-07-14). Raise this promptly and check the actual invoice date, since a closed window does not change the disposition.
