# SAGAR-JUL-1 · SG-7009

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹200.00 overbilled against contract
**What happened:** The carrier billed ₹1,680.00 for SG-7009, but the contract amount under §1 is ₹1,480.00 (weight ₹1,200.00 plus distance ₹280.00).
**Contract basis:** §1: freight is "a weight component of ₹6.00 per kg... and a distance component of ₹2.00 per km."
**Recommended action:** Ask the carrier to rebill at ₹1,480.00 and credit the ₹200.00 overcharge.
