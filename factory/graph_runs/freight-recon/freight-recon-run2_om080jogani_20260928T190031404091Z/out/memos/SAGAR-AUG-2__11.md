# SAGAR-AUG-2 · SG-7086

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹40.00 arithmetic error
**What happened:** The invoice's own components for SG-7086 (freight ₹390.00) sum to ₹390.00, matching the contract amount, but the line was billed at ₹430.00.
**Contract basis:** §1: freight is "a weight component of ₹6.00 per kg... and a distance component of ₹2.00 per km," which computes to ₹390.00 here.
**Recommended action:** Ask the carrier to correct the line total to ₹390.00 and credit the ₹40.00 difference.
