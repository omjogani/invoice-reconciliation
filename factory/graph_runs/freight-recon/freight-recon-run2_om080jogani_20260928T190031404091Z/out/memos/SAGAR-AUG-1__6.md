# SAGAR-AUG-1 · SG-7056

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹104.00 balance remaining after a partial credit
**What happened:** The carrier billed ₹1,940.00 for SG-7056 against a contract amount of ₹1,680.00, an overbill of ₹260.00. Credit note SAGAR-CN-01 corrected only ₹156.00 of that overbill, leaving ₹104.00 open.
**Contract basis:** §6: "The Carrier may issue credit notes correcting earlier invoices... and must cover the full corrected amount." The partial credit does not satisfy this.
**Recommended action:** Ask the carrier to issue a further credit note for the outstanding ₹104.00 to fully correct SAGAR-AUG-1.
