# FALCON-2026-07A · FF-8011

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹1,200.00 unauthorised detention charge
**What happened:** The invoice includes a ₹1,200.00 detention charge at consignee. The contract's accessorial schedule does not list detention charges, and no rate confirmation authorising one is on file.
**Contract basis:** §5 — "No other accessorial charges are payable under this agreement. Any service outside this schedule requires the Customer's written agreement in advance, evidenced by a rate confirmation referencing this agreement."
**Recommended action:** Ask Falcon to remove the ₹1,200.00 detention charge, or provide the written rate confirmation authorising it.
**Deadline:** The dispute window closes 2026-08-28 and is closed as of 2026-09-29 (the invoice date is not printed; the date assumes the end of the billing period, 2026-07-14). A closed window doesn't change the disposition — raise this promptly with the carrier and confirm the actual invoice date.
