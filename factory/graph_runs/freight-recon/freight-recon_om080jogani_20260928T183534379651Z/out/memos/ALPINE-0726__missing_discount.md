# ALPINE-0726 · Missing volume discount

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹1,007.46 discount shortfall on determinable lines
**What happened:** Alpine tendered 40 consignments in July 2026, well over the 12-consignment threshold that triggers a monthly invoice discount under §5. The invoice was billed with ₹0.00 discount applied instead of the required 5%.
**Contract basis:** §5 — "In any calendar month in which the Customer tenders more than 12 consignments, the Carrier will apply a 5% discount to the invoice total for that month's invoice."
**Recommended action:** Bill Alpine for the missing discount of ₹1,007.46 on the determinable lines. A further amount depends on line ALPINE-0726#5, which is being escalated separately for a contract-gap rate question; once that line settles, the total discount owed may increase.
