# ALPINE-0726 · AE-3005

**Disposition:** Escalate
**Amount:** ₹475.00 billed amount, unresolved pending contract clarification
**What happened:** The shipment's chargeable weight is exactly 50.0 kg. It was billed at ₹9.50/kg, the "under 50 kg" band, but the contract's two rate bands do not say which one governs a shipment weighing exactly 50 kg.
**Contract basis:** §2 — "consignments **under 50 kg**: ₹9.50 per kg of chargeable weight - consignments **over 50 kg**: ₹8.25 per kg of chargeable weight." Neither band explicitly covers exactly 50 kg.
**Recommended action:** Ask the contract owner to clarify which band applies at exactly 50 kg. The candidate amounts under the adjacent bands are ₹475.00 (under-50kg rate) and ₹412.50 (over-50kg rate). Hold the line pending that clarification rather than accepting the billed amount.
