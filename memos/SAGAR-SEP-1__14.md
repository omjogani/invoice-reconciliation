# SAGAR-SEP-1 · SG-7114

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹45.00 overbilled against contract
**What happened:** The carrier billed ₹505.00 for SG-7114, but the contract amount under §1 is ₹460.00 (weight ₹270.00 plus distance ₹190.00).
**Contract basis:** §1: freight is "a weight component of ₹6.00 per kg... and a distance component of ₹2.00 per km."
**Recommended action:** Ask the carrier to rebill at ₹460.00 and credit the ₹45.00 overcharge.
