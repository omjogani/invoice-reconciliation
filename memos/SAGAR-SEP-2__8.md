# SAGAR-SEP-2 · SG-7133

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹40.00 arithmetic error
**What happened:** SG-7133 was a cold-chain shipment. Its own components (freight ₹590.00 plus a cold-chain premium of ₹118.00) sum to ₹708.00, matching the contract amount, but the line was billed at ₹748.00.
**Contract basis:** §1 sets freight at "₹6.00 per kg... and ₹2.00 per km"; §3 adds "a premium of 20% on the freight charge" for cold chain, together totaling ₹708.00.
**Recommended action:** Ask the carrier to correct the line total to ₹708.00 and credit the ₹40.00 difference.
