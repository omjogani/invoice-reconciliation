# SAGAR-SEP-2 · SG-7133

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹40.00 overbilled due to arithmetic error
**What happened:** The line's billed components (₹590.00 freight + ₹118.00 cold chain premium) sum to ₹708.00, matching the contract, but the invoice prints a line total of ₹748.00 — a ₹40.00 addition error.
**Contract basis:** §1 sets the freight formula; §3 adds a "premium of 20% on the freight charge" for cold chain consignments, correctly computed here as ₹118.00.
**Recommended action:** Ask Sagar Roadlines to correct the arithmetic error and credit ₹40.00 against SAGAR-SEP-2.
