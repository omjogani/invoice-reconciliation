# SAGAR-JUL-1 · SG-7009

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹200.00 overbilled against the contract rate
**What happened:** The line was billed at ₹1,680.00, but the §1 contract formula applied to the shipment's weight and distance yields ₹1,480.00.
**Contract basis:** §1: freight is "a weight component of ₹6.00 per kg... and a distance component of ₹2.00 per km."
**Recommended action:** Ask Sagar Roadlines to correct the rate applied and credit ₹200.00 against SAGAR-JUL-1.
