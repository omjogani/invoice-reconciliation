# SAGAR-AUG-2 · SG-7086

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹40.00 overbilled due to arithmetic error
**What happened:** The line's billed components sum to ₹390.00, matching the §1 contract rate, but the invoice prints a line total of ₹430.00 — a ₹40.00 addition error.
**Contract basis:** §1: freight is "a weight component of ₹6.00 per kg... and a distance component of ₹2.00 per km," which correctly totals ₹390.00 here.
**Recommended action:** Ask Sagar Roadlines to correct the arithmetic error and credit ₹40.00 against SAGAR-AUG-2.
