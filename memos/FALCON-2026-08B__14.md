# FALCON-2026-08B · FF-8067

**Disposition:** Dispute
**Amount:** ₹1,200.00 unauthorised detention charge
**What happened:** The invoice includes a ₹1,200.00 detention charge at consignee alongside correctly billed freight and residential-delivery charges. The contract's accessorial schedule does not permit detention charges.
**Contract basis:** §5 — "No other accessorial charges are payable under this agreement. Any service outside this schedule requires the Customer's written agreement in advance, evidenced by a rate confirmation referencing this agreement."
**Recommended action:** Ask Falcon to remove the ₹1,200.00 detention charge, or provide the written rate confirmation authorising it.
**Deadline:** The dispute window closes 2026-10-14 and is open as of 2026-09-29 (the invoice date is not printed; the date assumes the end of the billing period, 2026-08-30). Raise now and confirm the actual invoice date.
